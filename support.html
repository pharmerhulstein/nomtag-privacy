Common questions

  • Which printers work? NomTag prints 50 x 30 mm labels over Bluetooth to the Niimbot B1. A printer is optional: you can build recipes and preview labels without one.
  • The printer is not found. Turn the printer on, keep it close to the phone, and allow Bluetooth for NomTag in Settings.
  • A scan does not log anything. Allow Camera for NomTag in Settings. Barcode labels need the saved recipe on the same phone. QR labels work on any phone with NomTag, with no saved recipe needed.
  • Apple Health. NomTag only writes calories, protein, fat, and carbs to Apple Health, and only if you allow it. Removing an entry in the Today tab also removes it from Health.
  • Sync code. Optional. It keeps saved recipes on all your devices. NomTag works without it.
