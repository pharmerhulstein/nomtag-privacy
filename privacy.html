NomTag Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

NomTag is a meal-prep label maker. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

What stays on your device

  • Your recipes, ingredients, and settings are stored on your device.
  • Your Today log (items you scan and log) is stored only on your device and kept for 45 days.
  • Camera: used only to scan a NomTag barcode or QR code label. Images are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Bluetooth: used only to connect to your label printer and send it the label to print.

Apple Health

If you allow it, NomTag writes calories, protein, fat, and carbohydrates to Apple Health when you log a scanned meal. It deletes those entries from Health if you remove the item in NomTag. NomTag does not use Health data for advertising or marketing, and does not share it with anyone.

Optional sync

If you create a sync code, your saved recipes and the first name you enter are sent to our server so they can sync across your devices. Anyone who has your code can see those recipes, so treat it like a password. Without a sync code, nothing is sent to the server.

Optional feedback

If you send feedback, we receive your message, your browser and screen size, your name (if you use a sync code), the recipe you are working on (only if you tick the box), and the email or phone number you choose to give us.

What we do not do

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • No sale or sharing of your data.
  • No accounts or passwords.

Retention and deletion

To delete synced data or feedback, email us at the address below and include your sync code. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.

Children

NomTag is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
